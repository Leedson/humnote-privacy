ChatFlick

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026
Developer: Leedson

This policy explains how ChatFlick, the Android app developed by Leedson, handles information when you create fictional chat videos. It applies to the app identified by the package name com.chatreel.

Your projects are processed on your device. ChatFlick does not require an account, operate a cloud service for your content, or automatically upload your projects, photos, or videos to us.

Information used by the app

ChatFlick accesses and stores the content you choose to enter or select so it can save projects, display previews, and create videos. This includes:

  • Project titles, participant names, fictional message text, reveal text, and animation or sound settings.
  • Photos you select for messages, participant avatars, or other project content. The app creates resized local copies for use in your projects.
  • Project identifiers, saved-project timestamps, temporary export files, and completed videos.

This information is processed locally. The app does not read your real SMS messages, messaging-app conversations, contacts, location, or microphone. Its chat and notification effects are created from project content; it does not read your device notifications.

Collection, tracking, and advertising

ChatFlick does not send personal information or project content to developer-operated servers. The app does not include advertising, analytics, tracking, or automatic crash-reporting SDKs. We do not sell your personal information. The Android release does not request internet access.

Photos and device storage

You select images through Android's system photo or document picker. ChatFlick accesses the images you select rather than requesting broad access to your photo library. If you choose an image from a cloud provider offered by the system picker, that provider handles retrieving the image under its own privacy policy.

Projects and imported photo copies are stored in the app's private storage. Completed videos are saved to the device's shared media storage, normally in Movies/ChatFlick, where gallery apps and other apps with appropriate access may see them.

Sharing and other services

When you choose to share an exported video, ChatFlick opens Android's share sheet and grants the app you select access to that video. The video can contain the text, names, and photos included in your project. The receiving app or service handles the video according to its own privacy policy.

Your gallery, device backup service, or other apps may independently sync exported videos if you have enabled those services. Google Play and Android may also process information when distributing, updating, or operating apps under their own policies. ChatFlick does not control these services.

Retention and deletion

  • Projects: Saved projects remain on your device until you delete them in ChatFlick or remove the app's local data.
  • Imported photos: Deleting a project does not immediately remove every imported photo copy. The app attempts to remove unused copies during a later startup once those files are more than 24 hours old. Copies referenced by other saved projects are retained.
  • Temporary files: The app attempts to remove temporary export files after processing and during startup cleanup. Interrupted operations may leave files until cleanup or removal of app data.
  • All private app data: You can remove local projects, imported copies, and cache through Android Settings → Apps → ChatFlick → Storage → Clear storage (wording varies by device), or by uninstalling the app.
  • Exported and shared videos: Deleting projects, clearing app storage, or uninstalling does not necessarily delete videos saved to shared storage or copies held by other apps or recipients. Delete these separately using your gallery, file manager, or the receiving service.

ChatFlick has no user accounts or developer-hosted project database. We cannot retrieve, restore, or remotely delete your local projects. The app disables standard Android app backup in its configuration; device migration behavior may vary by Android version and device manufacturer.

Security

ChatFlick relies on Android's app storage isolation to limit other apps' access to private project data. It does not add its own password protection or app-level encryption to projects. Exported videos are intentionally placed in shared media storage. Protect your device and consider what your videos reveal before sharing them. No storage method can guarantee absolute security.

Privacy questions and support

Contact Leedson at leedsonabraham@gmail.com for privacy questions. If you email us, your email address, message, and any attachments are processed by our email provider and used to respond to your request. Please send only information needed for your inquiry. Support correspondence is retained only as needed to address the inquiry and any applicable legal obligations; you may request its deletion using the same address.

Children's privacy

ChatFlick's local processing practices apply to all users. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information through the app. If a child sends personal information directly to us through support, a parent or guardian may contact us to request deletion.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when ChatFlick's features or data practices change. Updates will appear on this page with a revised date. Where required, we will provide additional notice or request consent before introducing new data practices.
