HumNote Privacy Policy

Effective September 30, 2026 · Developer: Leedson

Microphone and audio

HumNote uses microphone access to record humming when you start a recording. Audio is processed in memory on your device to detect musical notes. The app does not upload audio or save raw recordings as files. Temporary audio may remain in app memory until it is replaced or the app process ends. You can revoke microphone permission in Android Settings.

Saved melodies

When you save a melody, its name, creation date, and musical note data are stored locally on your device. You can delete saved melodies in My Melodies. Clearing app storage or uninstalling the app removes its locally stored data. HumNote does not provide cloud backup or account synchronization.

Collection and sharing

This version of HumNote does not transmit your recordings or melodies to Leedson or third parties. It has no account registration, advertisements, or analytics SDK. Google Play and Android may separately process information under their own policies.

Contact and changes

If you email leetheleader@gmail.com, Leedson receives the email address and information you choose to send and uses it to respond to your request. Do not include recordings or sensitive information unless necessary. Contact the same address to request deletion of support correspondence. This policy may be updated when the app changes; the current effective date is shown above.
